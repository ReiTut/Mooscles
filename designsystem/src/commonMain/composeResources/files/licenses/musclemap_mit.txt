The Mooscles body map is adapted from MuscleMap (https://github.com/Jsplice/MuscleMap):
the path data of packages/assets/src/male-front.ts and male-back.ts at commit
170cfacda79376dd59ac0cf2f7dfab8ff9f4443a (2026-09-15). The paths were grouped into
17 regions and drawn in the app's colors; the geometry is unchanged. The paths are
vectors traced by hand in Inkscape, as published at that commit; the reference
photographs they were traced over are not included. The MuscleMap license follows.
