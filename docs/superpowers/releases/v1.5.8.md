# Mooscles 1.5.8 · Build 30

- Fixed the **Records** list and PR total on the workout share preview and exported PNGs. A reps record at a lighter load stays in workout history. The share card uses the exercise's record load when deciding which reps records to include.
- In the reproduced workout, **One-arm cable lateral raise** still shows **15 kg × 3** as its best set. The prior record was **15 kg × 5**, so the card omits the **5 kg × 8** entry from Records and shows **4 PRs**. Every exported page shows the workout-wide total.

This full release distributes the **Android debug APK** with the original Mooscles signing certificate. Android may ask you to approve installation.
